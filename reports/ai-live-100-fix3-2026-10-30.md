## Root-cause fixes

1. **Long hostile input:** split unsupported HTML, role-impersonating JSON, URLs, repeated strings, emoji and invisible Unicode from deterministic product search extraction. Preserve a product mentioned before isolated hostile instructions instead of allowing an LLM classifier to turn the request into general Q&A.
2. **Incorrect gold label:** R2-208 asks for a 14-inch LG laptop under 1m KRW. The available LG gram 15 test fixture is 15-inch and over budget; it cannot be an acceptable answer.
3. **Hard display eligibility:** for exact laptop-diagonal questions, require advertised evidence of the requested size and brand before including a card. Unknown screen size is **not** assumed to match. This trades recall for strict factual safety and needs merchant-catalog coverage monitoring.
4. **Unverifiable price injection:** when a named product precedes a command to invent unobserved prices, retain only the named product as the lookup subject; the existing server price/identity fact gates remain in force.
5. **Truthful denominator:** `modelEvaluated`, `modelNotRun`, `modelPass` and `modelFail` are reported separately from all 100 application cases.
